Terms of Service

Payment:

For orders under $30, full upfront payment is required.

For orders over $30, payment is split 50/50: Once all details are confirmed, you send the first 50%. When the initial result is ready and you're happy with it, you send the remaining 50% to receive the high-res file without watermarks.

Revisions:

You get 2 free revisions during the sketch stage, as it’s easy to change the pose, expression, hair, or outfit at this point.

If you say, "I approved the sketch, but now at the final render stage I want to change the pose completely or swap the character"—that’s not a revision, that’s a redraw. Any major changes requested after the sketch is approved will cost an extra $10–20.

Refund Policy

If cancelled by ME:

100% full refund at any time if I get sick, run out of time, or can't finish the art piece for any reason.

If cancelled by the CLIENT:

Before work begins (before the sketch stage): 100% refund, no questions asked.

During the sketch stage: 50% refund (since time was already spent on the initial draft).

After sketch approval (lineart/render stage): 0% refund. My time and effort have already been put into the work.

Turnaround Time

I don't box myself into "done in 1 day" deadlines. The clock starts once I receive the upfront payment and all character references.

Standard TAT: 3–14 days (or faster, depending on the queue).

Rush Orders: If you need it done urgently within 24 hours, a rush fee applies (e.g., +50% of the total price, but negotiable).
