🎨 Technical Info

1. I try to prioritize work wisely. I don't see a difference between a simple request like "draw this character in this pose" and a complex one with every tiny detail spelled out. I'll pick the commission I know I can execute better and faster without wasting time, or simply whichever one is earlier in line.
2. Prices may vary depending on whether details are added or removed.
3. Keep in mind that I'm not a pro with 10 years of experience. My skill level can also affect the pricing to some extent.
4. Don't be shy to talk to me! We can easily negotiate a small discount, or talk about fixing and adding details to the final drawing/sketch.
